# Microsoft Corporation

## Company profile

Microsoft is a technology company that develops software, cloud services,
devices, gaming products, and productivity tools.

## Major business segments

Microsoft's major reported business areas include Productivity and Business
Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing.

- **Productivity and Business Processes:** Productivity software, business
  applications, collaboration products, and professional networking services.
- **Intelligent Cloud:** Cloud infrastructure, server products, enterprise
  services, and related platforms.
- **More Personal Computing:** Windows, devices, gaming, search, advertising,
  and related consumer products.
