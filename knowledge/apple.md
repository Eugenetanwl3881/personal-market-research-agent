# Apple Inc.

## Company profile

Apple is a technology company focused on consumer hardware, software, and
services. Its products are distributed through direct retail, online, and
third-party channels.

## Major business segments

Apple's major reported business areas include iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables,
Home and Accessories, and Services.

- **iPhone:** Apple's smartphone product line and related operating system.
- **Mac:** Personal computers and related software.
- **iPad:** Tablet computers and related accessories.
- **Wearables, Home and Accessories:** Products such as Apple Watch, AirPods,
  and other connected devices and accessories.
- **Services:** Digital services and subscriptions, including the App Store,
  cloud services, payments, and other content services.
